How to play

One button

Space / ↑ / W, click, or tap to flap. Each flap gives instant lift. That is the whole control scheme.

Gates

Pillars drift toward you. Pass through the glowing gap. Touching an edge, the sky ceiling, or the sea ends the flight.

Scoring

Each gate: 100. Near-center pass: up to +50. Consecutive centered passes build a streak worth up to +100 per gate. Finish under par for a time bonus.

Keyboard

Esc/P pause · U undo (practice) · R restart · H help · menus work with Tab and Enter.

Gamepad

A/Cross flaps, Start pauses, shoulder buttons and D-pad navigate menus.

Fair play

Every round is seeded and replayable. Daily skies are identical for everyone on the same UTC day. Assists are declared with your score.
